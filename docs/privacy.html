FieldSense

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

The short version: FieldSense does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. Your fields are saved only on your device.

Information we do not collect

FieldSense has no accounts or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or payment information. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Your fields stay on your device

The fields you add (name, location, crop, soil type, planting date, and acreage) are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting a field in the app, or deleting the app, removes that information.

How location is used

To give you a daily verdict, FieldSense needs your field's location. You can let the app detect your current location (with your permission) or search for a town or county instead.

When the app refreshes a field, it sends that field's coordinates, crop, soil type, and planting date to the FieldSense server. The server uses them to get weather data and calculate your verdict, then returns the result. This information is processed in real time and is not saved to a database or linked to you.

You can turn off location access at any time in your iPhone's Settings and use the town or county search instead.

Weather and location search

Weather data and location search are provided by Open-Meteo. To answer a request, Open-Meteo receives the coordinates of the field or the town name you search for. It does not receive your name or any account information. See Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy information for how it handles requests.

Children

FieldSense is a general-audience farming tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above.
