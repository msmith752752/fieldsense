Common questions

Nothing happens when I tap "Get My Field Intelligence."
Make sure a location is set first. Tap Detect to use your current location, or tap Search a town or county and pick a result. You'll see a green "Location set" box when it's ready.

Location detection isn't working.
Check that location access is allowed in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → FieldSense. You can also use the town or county search instead.

How is the daily verdict calculated?
FieldSense combines recent rainfall (1, 3, 7, and 14 days), the 7-day forecast, your soil's drainage, and your crop's thresholds to judge whether conditions are workable today.

Why should I add a planting date?
It lets FieldSense track your crop's growth stage, such as Germination or Tasseling / Silking.

Where are my fields saved?
Only on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Where does the weather data come from?
Weather data is provided by Open-Meteo.com.
